Three roads. One stronger hunter.

Choose how you actually like to play. Every path below is PvE.

Start with Bountiful Delves and Nightmare Prey for dependable solo-friendly Vault progression. The Labyrinth is another exciting route.

✶SOLO-FRIENDLY

The lone ranger

Progress without committing to a raid schedule or dungeon group.

  • Tier 8+ Bountiful Delves: Hero-track Great Vault potential (305 at Tier 8+).

  • Tier 11 Delves / Labyrinth: tougher challenges, higher rewards, and opportunities to collect powerful gear.
  • Showdown / outdoor weeklies: useful catch-up gear and currencies.

How do I actually upgrade gear? +

Speak to Cuzolth, the item-upgrade NPC at approximately 48.6, 62 in Silvermoon City. Place your item in the upgrade interface. Each step normally uses 20 matching Mistcrests plus a little gold.

Slot high-watermark rule: once you've reached an item level in a slot, upgrades to that level on a replacement item are free of additional Mistcrests. Think of upgrading your slot, not just one particular item.

Do not over-invest in low-track gear if you expect a stronger drop shortly. Higher-content loot often starts several upgrade steps ahead.

What are the best weekly Vault breakpoints? +

The Great Vault is in Silvermoon's bank. You choose one weekly item from the unlocked options. Dungeon choices require 1 / 4 / 8 qualifying dungeons, Raid choices 2 / 4 / 6 bosses, and World choices 2 / 4 / 8 world activities or Delves.

Helpful targets: a Tier 8+ Bountiful Delve gives a 305 Hero-track World Vault reward; a +10 Mythic+ provides a 318 Myth-track Dungeon Vault option. Higher rewards are subject to the category and activity rules; check the in-game Vault tooltip.

SPENDING MISTCRESTS

Upgrade in this order.

  1. 01Ranged weaponMajor damage impact
  2. 02TrinketsEspecially strong effects
  3. 03Helm / chest / legsHigh-stat armor slots
  4. 04Shoulders / gloves / belt / bootsSolid secondary upgrades
  5. 05Rings / neck / cloak / bracersFinish lower-priority slots

Solo pet tip

Let your pet hold enemies, heal with Mend Pet, and use defensive cooldowns proactively. Pick the pet family and utility that suit the encounter.

✢

Dungeon tip

Turn pet Growl off in groups; keep Counter Shot and traps ready. You can maintain damage while moving, so prioritize mechanics.

◇

Don't obsess over percentages

Higher item level, a great trinket, and tier bonuses often beat a smaller item's “ideal” secondary stats. Sim close choices.

07 / RETURNING FROM THE WAR WITHIN

What changed while you were away?

Only the updates that matter for a returning level 90 Hunter.

01

Midnight raised the cap to 90

You're now playing the second Worldsoul Saga expansion, with Silvermoon as a major hub. Old expansion gear quickly gives way to new seasonal rewards.

02

Numbers got squished

Item levels and stats are drastically smaller than in The War Within. The Season 2 ladder runs from Adventurer 266–282 to Myth 318–334.

03

Crests got simpler

Upgrade costs are mostly a matching Mistcrest plus gold. No Valorstones. The October 20 update lifted the seasonal crest cap.

04

More solo ways to gear

Prey, updated Delves, the new Labyrinth of Kindo'jan, and world Showdown activities widen your PvE options.

05

Tier & bonus rolls evolved

The Catalyst preserves the source item's stats. Season 2 introduced Nebulous Voidcores, and 12.1.5 added an extra route through Orin Straylight.

06

A new power-up arrived

Collect 10 Ascendant Venomstones from qualifying higher-end content to raise eligible fully upgraded Season 2 weapons, trinkets, or necks, up to 340.

NEW IN 12.1.5OCTOBER 13, 2026

One more thing: check in with Orin.

In Silvermoon, progress the In the Catalyst's Shadow → Prismatic Potential → Umbral Blessings of the Catalyst questline to unlock an additional weekly Nebulous Voidcore purchase option. Follow with Ancient Depths, Ancient Venom → Ascending with Venomstones to unlock Ascendant Venomstone rewards.

Venomstones can come from Heroic/Mythic raid bosses, +10 or higher Mythic+ keys, Tier 11 Bountiful Delves, and Nightmare Prey Champion chests. Upgrade qualifying fully upgraded Hero/Myth or maximum-quality Tidal crafted weapons, trinkets, or necks near the Catalyst. For a BM Hunter, favor the weapon → trinkets → neck.
